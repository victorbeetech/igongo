# Cleaner (public areas)

**Level:** Frontline staff · **Role key:** `cleaner` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Cleans public areas, offices, washrooms and grounds paths on a schedule.

**Reports to:** [Housekeeping Supervisor](housekeeping-supervisor.md)

**Uses:** The Igongo app on a phone

## Home screen tiles

- My cleaning round
- Checklist
- Report a fault

Includes everything in the [Every Staff Member (base role)](staff-base.md) role, plus:
