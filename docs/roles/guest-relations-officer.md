# Guest Relations Officer

**Level:** Supervisors and officers · **Role key:** `guest-relations-officer` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Looks after guests' happiness: VIPs, feedback, complaints and loyalty.

**Reports to:** [Front Office Manager](front-office-manager.md)

**Uses:** The Igongo app on a phone

## Home screen tiles

- VIPs in house
- Complaints open
- Low ratings today
- Birthdays this week
- Loyalty sign-ups

- Complaint vouchers up to Tier 1.

## Extra rules

- Can switch to the client view at any time to see the app exactly as a guest sees it.
