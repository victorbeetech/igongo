# Social Media and Content Officer

**Level:** Supervisors and officers · **Role key:** `social-media-officer` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Posts on social media, answers reviews and runs campaigns.

**Reports to:** [Sales & Marketing Manager](sales-marketing-manager.md)

**Uses:** The Igongo app on a phone

## Home screen tiles

- Campaign results
- Review scores
- Active promotions
