# Health and Safety Officer

**Level:** Supervisors and officers · **Role key:** `health-safety-officer` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Runs safety inspections, fire drills and incident follow-up.

**Reports to:** [General Manager (GM)](general-manager.md)

**Uses:** The Igongo app on a phone

## Home screen tiles

- Inspections due
- Incidents open
- Open faults by urgency

## Modules, screens and default permissions

Includes everything in the [Every Staff Member (base role)](staff-base.md) role, plus:

| Module | Actions | Data scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Inspections & Checklists](../modules/39-inspections.md) | view, create, edit, approve | Everything |
| [Security, Patrols & Incidents](../modules/45-security-incidents.md) | view, edit, approve | Everything |
| [Faults & Maintenance](../modules/40-faults-maintenance.md) | view, create | Everything |
| [Training & Certificates](../modules/34-training.md) | view, create | Everything |

## Approval limits

- None. This role does not approve anything.

## Extra rules

- Can switch to the client view at any time to see the app exactly as a guest sees it.
