# Sales Executive

**Level:** Supervisors and officers · **Role key:** `sales-executive` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Finds and looks after corporate, event and group clients.

**Reports to:** [Sales & Marketing Manager](sales-marketing-manager.md)

**Uses:** The Igongo app on a phone

## Home screen tiles

- My leads
- My corporate accounts
- Quotes waiting
- Events I sold

## Modules, screens and default permissions

Includes everything in the [Every Staff Member (base role)](staff-base.md) role, plus:

| Module | Actions | Data scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Corporate Accounts](../modules/55-corporate-accounts.md) | view, create, edit | Assigned to them |
| [CRM: Guest & Client Profiles](../modules/54-crm-guests.md) | view, create, edit | Assigned to them |
| [Events & Conferences](../modules/69-events-conferences.md) | view, create | Assigned to them |
| [Museum & Cultural Visits](../modules/68-museum.md) | view, create | Assigned to them |
| [Receipts & Invoicing](../modules/78-receipts-invoicing.md) | view, create | Assigned to them |

## Approval limits

- None. Discounts go to the Sales & Marketing Manager.

## Extra rules

- Can switch to the client view at any time to see the app exactly as a guest sees it.
