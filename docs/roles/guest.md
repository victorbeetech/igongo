# Guest / Client

**Level:** Guests and clients · **Role key:** `guest` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Any visitor or customer: hotel guests, diners, museum visitors, swimmers, spa clients, shoppers and event guests. This is the client view that staff can also switch to.

**Uses:** The Igongo app (same app and same entry point as staff), the website and WhatsApp

## Home screen tiles

- Book a room
- Order food (delivery, dine-in, takeaway or pre-order)
- Book a table
- Museum and tours
- Events
- Pool passes
- Spa
- Shop
- My bookings and orders
- Chat with us
- My receipts
- My points
