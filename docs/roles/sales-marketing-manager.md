# Sales & Marketing Manager

**Level:** Department managers · **Role key:** `sales-marketing-manager` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Brings in business: corporate clients, packages, campaigns, promotions, website content and reviews.

**Reports to:** [General Manager (GM)](general-manager.md)

**Uses:** Web portal and Igongo app

## Home screen tiles

- Leads and enquiries
- Corporate accounts
- Campaign results
- Active promotions
- Review scores

## Approval limits

- Promotions up to Tier 2.

## Extra rules

- Campaigns go only to guests who agreed to receive them.
- Can switch to the client view at any time to see the app exactly as a guest sees it.
