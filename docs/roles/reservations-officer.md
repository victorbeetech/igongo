# Reservations Officer

**Level:** Supervisors and officers · **Role key:** `reservations-officer` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Handles room, table, museum, pool, spa and event bookings from all channels.

**Reports to:** [Front Office Manager](front-office-manager.md)

**Uses:** The Igongo app on a phone

## Home screen tiles

- New bookings
- Bookings waiting for APEX
- Deposits due
- Enquiries
- WhatsApp chats
