# Delivery Dispatcher

**Level:** Supervisors and officers · **Role key:** `dispatcher` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Assigns deliveries to riders and watches them until delivered.

**Reports to:** [Delivery & Transport Manager](logistics-manager.md)

**Uses:** The Igongo app on a phone

## Home screen tiles

- Orders ready for pickup
- Live rider map
- Late deliveries
- Riders online

## Modules, screens and default permissions

Includes everything in the [Every Staff Member (base role)](staff-base.md) role, plus:

| Module | Actions | Data scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Delivery & Riders](../modules/66-delivery-riders.md) | view, create, edit | Everything |
| [Food Ordering](../modules/62-food-ordering.md) | view | Everything |
| [Cash-up & Reconciliation](../modules/79-cash-up.md) | view | Own department |

## Approval limits

- None.

## Extra rules

- Can switch to the client view at any time to see the app exactly as a guest sees it.
