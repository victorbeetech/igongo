# Chief Executive Officer

**Level:** Leadership · **Role key:** `ceo` · [All roles](README.md)

> A pre-made role. The super admin can rename it, change every permission, tile and limit below, clone it, or delete it, all without code.

## Purpose

Leads the whole business, sets direction, and approves the largest decisions below the board.

**Reports to:** [Board of Directors](board-of-directors.md)

**Uses:** Igongo app and web portal

## Home screen tiles

- Business dashboard
- Approvals waiting for me
- Department daily digest
- Guest satisfaction
- Cash position
- Notice board

## Approval limits

- Up to Tier 3 for spending, advances, bonuses and discounts.
- Approves senior hires and exits.

## Extra rules

- 2FA required.
- Can switch to the client view at any time to see the app exactly as a guest sees it.
